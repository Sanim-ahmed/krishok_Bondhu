Fertilizer Recommendations

Crop Fertilizer Application Time
Rice Urea, TSP, MoP Basal dose + 15 days after planting
Wheat Urea, TSP, Gypsum Basal dose + 21 days after sowing
Potato DAP, Urea, MOP Basal dose + 30 days after planting
Tomato DAP, Urea, MOP Basal dose + 20 days after transplanting
Onion DAP, Urea, MOP Basal dose + 30 days after planting
Brinjal DAP, Urea, MOP, Compost Basal dose + 25 days after transplanting
Chili DAP, Urea, Compost Basal dose + 30 days after transplanting
Jute Urea, TSP, Gypsum Basal dose + 20 days after sowing
Mustard DAP, Urea, Boron Basal dose + 25 days after sowing
Lentil TSP, MoP, Urea All at basal dose before sowing
